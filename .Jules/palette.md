## 2024-09-17 - Button loading states and missing ARIA labels
**Learning:** Found several buttons in `track-order.html` and `odorstrike.html` that lack loading states and `aria-label` attributes, which impacts accessibility and interaction feedback.
**Action:** Enhance UX by adding loading text or spinner, disabling buttons during async operations, and adding `aria-labels` to buttons like "Remove from cart" (`cart-remove`).
