About · Smelloff

About Smelloff.

Smelloff is a Hyderabad-based consumer brand with one product and one job: ODORSTRIKE — a pocket-sized fabric odor remover spray. 50ml, ₹229, cash on delivery, ships pan-India.

Why we exist

Deodorant works on skin. But by 3pm on an Indian summer day, it's not your skin that smells — it's your shirt. Sweat compounds like isovaleric and butyric acid bind to fabric, especially polyester, and no amount of deodorant, perfume, or re-washing reliably fixes a shirt mid-day.

That gap — a fabric-specific fix, designed for Indian heat, humidity, and commutes, at an accessible price — simply didn't exist. So we built it.

The founders

Smelloff was founded by Jogdhande Nikhil Patil (founder) and Vineeth Kumar Duggepogu (co-founder). The two are friends who met at a job interview in Hyderabad and went on to work together for about a year and a half at Zomato Everyday, a cloud kitchen.

The idea started with a slightly embarrassing moment: Nikhil walking into a client meeting after an hour-long Ola ride in 41°C Hyderabad heat, and watching a colleague's expression shift. Six months of failed prototypes later — fancy fragrant water, unstable enzyme formulas — ODORSTRIKE's final formulation worked: Zinc PCA to neutralize odor molecules at the source, not mask them, paired with HPβCD (a cyclodextrin), the ring-shaped molecule that traps odor inside fabric.

What we believe

  • Neutralize, don't mask. Fragrance over sweat smells worse than either. ODORSTRIKE neutralizes the odor molecule itself.
  • Built for India. Formulated and tested in Hyderabad heat — for packed commutes, monsoon damp, and 11-hour office days in the same shirt.
  • No-risk buying. Cash on Delivery pan-India, free shipping on prepaid orders, and a straightforward returns policy.
  • Honest claims. Our guides explain the actual science of fabric odor — including when our product is not the right tool.

The product

ODORSTRIKE is a 50ml fabric-only mist. Two sprays on the collar or underarm fabric, and it dries clean in 15–30 seconds as active neutralizers target trapped sweat odor. It works on cotton, polyester, denim, and wool. It is not a perfume and not a deodorant — here's the difference.
