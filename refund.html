Eligibility for refund

  • Product returned within 7-day return window (see Returns Policy)
  • Order cancelled before dispatch
  • Damaged or wrong product delivered
  • Package lost in transit and not recoverable
  • Payment deducted but order not confirmed (auto-refund within 5–7 days by payment gateway)

Refund timeline

  • UPI / Cards / Netbanking: 5–7 business days after return is received and inspected
  • COD orders: 5–7 business days via bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) after we collect your account details
  • Cancellations before dispatch: refund initiated within 24 hours

Refund amount

The full product amount is refunded. Shipping is free on all orders, so nothing is deducted for it. Cash on Delivery orders carry a ₹89 handling charge (₹318 collected on delivery), shown at checkout before you order. That charge pays for collecting cash on delivery and is not refunded: a returned COD order is refunded ₹229 per bottle.

Partial refunds

ODORSTRIKE is sold as a single 50ml unit (₹229). If you ordered more than one bottle and return only some of them, a partial refund is processed for the returned units at their per-unit price. The units you keep are not affected.

How to track your refund

Once we initiate the refund, you'll receive a confirmation notification with the refund transaction reference ID. For bank/UPI refunds, the amount appears in your account based on your bank's processing SLA (usually 5–7 business days). If the refund is delayed beyond 10 business days, contact info@smelloff.in or WhatsApp +91 93929 74031.

Order cancellation

You can cancel any order before it is dispatched for a 100% full refund — see our Cancellation Policy. Once dispatched, the order cannot be cancelled in transit but can be returned post-delivery as per our Returns Policy.
