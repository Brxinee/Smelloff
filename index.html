The chemistry

Four layers do the heavy lifting.

01 — TRAP

HPβCD (Cyclodextrin)

A hollow ring-shaped molecule that physically cages odor molecules inside itself. The smell isn't covered — it's captured.

02 — NEUTRALIZE

Zinc PCA

A zinc salt that deactivates the captured odor compounds and shuts the smell down at the source.

03 — PREVENT

Triethyl Citrate

Slows the breakdown of fresh sweat before new odor can form — that's what keeps fabric protected for up to 8 hours.

04 — ANTI-REGROWTH

Zinc Gluconate

Keeps odor from re-developing in the fibres between wears, so the fix holds long after you spray.

How to use

Three seconds. No technique.

  1. 01
    Hold 15cm from fabric. Closer and you'll oversaturate. Further and the mist disperses before landing.
  2. 02
    Spray 2–3 pumps per zone. Focus on collar, armpits, chest, and crotch. Skip areas that aren't smelly — you're not perfuming, you're neutralizing.
  3. 03
    Wait 10 seconds. The mist flashes off. The actives bind to the odor. The fabric is dry, the smell is gone, and nobody knows you used it.

Questions
Is ODORSTRIKE safe on skin?

No. ODORSTRIKE is formulated exclusively for fabric — its solvent and odor actives are designed for fibres, not skin. Do not spray on skin, face, or eyes. If contact occurs, rinse with water.

Will it stain my clothes?

No. The formula is glycerine-free and dries fast with zero residue — nothing sticky is left behind, even on dark fabric. Works on cotton, polyester, denim, and wool. We recommend testing on a small hidden area first for delicate fabrics like silk.

How long does the protection last?

Up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office or commute conditions. After heavy gym sessions or long rides, re-spray worn areas. See the full system for staying fresh through an 11-hour office day.

How many uses per bottle?

Approximately 250 sprays per 50ml bottle. At 2–3 sprays per use, that's roughly 80–125 uses — enough for 2–3 months of daily use.

Does it work on dry-clean-only fabric?

We don't recommend it for dry-clean-only items, leather, or suede. Stick to machine-washable fabrics like cotton, polyester, denim, and wool.

Is the fragrance strong?

Very subtle. The fragrance is designed to be barely noticeable — just enough to signal freshness without announcing itself. It fades within a minute, leaving your clothes smelling neutral.
