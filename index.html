Questions, answered

What is Smelloff?

Smelloff is an Indian odor-control brand for men. It goes after smell where it hides — on clothes, shoes, helmets and gear — instead of covering it with fragrance.

Is Smelloff a deodorant or a perfume?

Neither. Deodorant works on skin. Smelloff works on the fabric, shoes and gear that skin touches all day. It completes your routine instead of replacing it.

What does Smelloff make?

The first product is ODORSTRIKE, a pocket fabric odor eliminator mist for clothes. Smelloff's scope covers odor on shoes, helmets and gear too.

Where is Smelloff made?

Made in India. Smelloff is based in Hyderabad and ships across India.

Does Smelloff go on skin?

No. Smelloff products are built for fabric and gear, not skin.
