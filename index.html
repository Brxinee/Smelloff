DETAILS & SPECIFICATIONS

Everything you need to know.

Formulation chemistry, textile compatibility, internal testing conditions, and shipping policies.

01 How to Use +

Application: Hold bottle 10–15cm away from target fabric zones (collars, underarm panels, back, or chest). Apply 2–3 light pumps as a fine mist.

Drying Time: Allow 30–60 seconds for the evaporative carrier to flash off and dry completely before putting on or stepping out.

Saturation Limit: Do not soak or oversaturate garment fibers. Designed as an on-the-go refresh between wears; not a substitute for laundering physically soiled clothing.

02 Fabric Safety & Compatibility +

Fabric Only: Formulated specifically for clothing textiles and wearable fabrics. Not intended for direct skin application or body spray use.

Everyday Textiles: Tested on everyday washable fabrics including cotton, polyester, athletic synthetics, denim, and cotton-poly blends.

Clean Finish: Designed to dry cleanly without white chalking, oily rings, or residue when applied as directed.

Delicate Materials: For pure silk, raw/unsealed leather, artisanal natural dyes, or vintage fabrics with uncertain colorfastness, patch test on an inconspicuous interior seam first.

03 Ingredients & Formulation +

Hydroxypropyl Beta-Cyclodextrin (HPβCD): A plant-derived ring-shaped oligosaccharide with a hydrophobic inner cavity that physically complexes and traps volatile odor compounds so they cannot release into ambient air.

Zinc PCA: A biocompatible zinc salt of pyrrolidone carboxylic acid that chemically binds and helps neutralize sweat-associated volatile fatty acid compounds on contact.

Evaporative Carrier: Purified water base combined with quick-evaporating isopropyl alcohol to carry active complexes into fiber weave and evaporate within 30–60 seconds.

Fragrance Profile: Trace hypoallergenic fragrance (<1%) providing a clean, subtle profile without heavy cologne trails. 0% propellant gases, 0% mineral oils, 0% bleaching agents.

04 Testing & Development +

Textile Matrix Testing: Evaluated across standard everyday clothing fabrics—cotton weave, activewear synthetics, denim, and poly-blends.

Real-World Stress Conditions: Formulated and tested under Indian heat and humidity conditions in Hyderabad to evaluate odor neutralization during active commutes and full workdays.

Observed Performance: Observed up to 8 hours of fabric odor protection under normal daily-wear conditions in internal product development testing.

Functional Scope: Works by trapping and neutralizing volatile odor compounds on fabric. It does not kill bacteria, sterilize textiles, or replace laundry detergent.
