How it works

Mist. Dry. Move.

Apply to clothing. Wait. Wear it.

  2. 02

Formula v3.1

Two hero actives. A four-layer system.

Confidence, not a chemistry lecture. These are not the only ingredients.

HPβCD

Trap

Hydroxypropyl-β-cyclodextrin. A ring-shaped molecule that holds volatile odor compounds in the fabric so they don’t lift back into the air.

Zinc PCA

Neutralize

A zinc salt that binds sweat-associated odor compounds on the textile. For clothing fibers — not a skin deodorant.

Before you buy

Common questions.

Can I use it on skin?

No. Fabric only. Not for skin, face, hair, or eyes. Keep deodorant for the body; this is for the clothes.

Will it replace washing?

No. It is a between-wash fabric refresh. It does not remove dirt, oils, or stains. Wash normally when the garment is soiled.

Will it leave marks?

ODORSTRIKE is water-soluble and dries clear — no white residue on dark fabric. Tested on black, navy, charcoal, and grey shirts across 30+ wash cycles: no marks, no stiffness, no colour change. Patch-test silk, zari, and heavy embroidery on a hidden seam first. Do not use on leather, suede, or dry-clean-only garments.

How does COD work?

Cash on delivery is ₹289 at the door (₹229 + ₹60 handling). COD orders are dispatched after phone confirmation. Prepaid UPI is ₹229 with free shipping, dispatched within 48 hours of confirmation.

Can I use deodorant on clothes?

Deodorant is for skin. Spraying it on a shirt can stain fabric and still miss odor already in the weave. ODORSTRIKE is the fabric job — clothes only, never skin.

Is ODORSTRIKE a perfume or fabric freshener?

No. It is a fabric odor eliminator, not a perfume and not a scented fabric freshener. A light scent is in the formula; the job is trapping and neutralizing odor in clothing, not covering it.
