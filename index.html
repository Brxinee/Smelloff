Questions, answered

Is ODORSTRIKE safe on skin?

Don't use it on skin. ODORSTRIKE is formulated exclusively for fabric — never skin, face, hair or body. Keep your regular deodorant for skin and use ODORSTRIKE on the clothes that trap sweat odor. If it touches skin, rinse with clean water.

Will it stain my clothes?

No. It is glycerine-free and dries clear with no stiff or powdery residue when sprayed from 15–20 cm. Safe on everyday washable fabrics like cotton, polyester, denim and wool blends. Patch-test silk, zari and delicate embroidery on a hidden seam first.

How long does it last?

Up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office and commute conditions. On very hot days or after a workout, reapply 1–2 sprays. One 50ml bottle gives about 250 sprays.

Does it replace washing?

No. It neutralizes odor trapped in clothing fibres but does not remove dirt, oils or stains. Think of it as an odor reset between wears, alongside your normal laundry.

Is COD available?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across India with a ₹60 handling charge — ₹289 collected for one bottle, against ₹229 prepaid with free shipping. COD orders are confirmed by a quick call before dispatch.

How long does shipping take?

Orders dispatch from Hyderabad within 48 hours of confirmation. Delivery takes 3–5 business days to major metros and 5–7 to tier 2 and tier 3 cities. Prepaid orders ship free nationwide.

What if I don't like it?

You have 7 days from delivery to return it. The bottle must be at least 80% full and in its original packaging. Reverse pickup is arranged where serviceable and refunds are processed within 5–7 business days. Returns policy.

Is the fragrance strong?

No. It has a light fresh fabric scent that settles close to the weave — it is not a perfume and won't fight with your cologne.
