What's in the bottle

Not perfume. Not body deodorant. Those sit on top of the fabric and add scent over the smell; ODORSTRIKE goes after the odor trapped in the fibres, so it is neutralized instead of masked.

CompositionFormula v3.1
  • Trap

    HPβCD

    A hollow ring-shaped molecule that cages odor compounds in the fibres, so less of the smell reaches your nose.

  • Neutralize

    Zinc PCA

    Helps keep odor from building back up in the fibres between wears, so the reset holds through the day.

What is ODORSTRIKE?

ODORSTRIKE is a clothing deodorant made by Smelloff in Hyderabad, India: a 50ml pocket fabric odor spray that traps and neutralizes sweat, food and smoke odor in the fabric instead of covering it with perfume, and leaves a light fresh scent. It goes on clothes, never on skin: it is not a body deodorant, not a perfume and not a room spray. It dries in 15–30 seconds and gives up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office/commute conditions.

Is ODORSTRIKE safe on skin?

Don't use it on skin. ODORSTRIKE is formulated exclusively for fabric — never skin, face, hair or body. Keep your regular deodorant for skin and use ODORSTRIKE on the clothes that trap sweat odor. If it touches skin, rinse with clean water.

Will it stain my clothes?

No. It is glycerine-free and dries clear with no stiff or powdery residue when sprayed from 15–20 cm. Safe on everyday washable fabrics like cotton, polyester, denim and wool blends. Patch-test silk, zari and delicate embroidery on a hidden seam first.

How do I use it?

Hold the bottle 15–20 cm away, spray 2–3 light mists directly onto odor-prone zones (underarms, collar, or chest), let the fine mist dry clean in 15–30 seconds, and wear. In high humidity, allow the fabric to dry fully before putting the garment on.

How long does the effect last?

Up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office and commute conditions. On very hot days or after a workout, reapply 1–2 sprays. One 50ml bottle gives about 250 sprays.

What fabrics can I use it on?

ODORSTRIKE works on everyday washable clothing fabrics including cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, wool, and synthetic blends. Do not use on leather, suede, or dry-clean-only garments. When in doubt on delicate fabrics, test a small inside seam first.

Does it replace washing?

No. It neutralizes odor trapped in clothing fibres but does not remove dirt, oils or stains. Think of it as an odor reset between wears, alongside your normal laundry.

Is the fragrance strong?

No. It has a light fresh fabric scent that settles close to the weave — it is not a perfume and won't fight with your cologne.
