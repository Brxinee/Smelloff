08 // Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about ODORSTRIKE.

Is ODORSTRIKE a perfume or body spray?
No. ODORSTRIKE is not a perfume, cologne, deodorant, or body spray. It is a dedicated fabric odor neutralizer designed specifically for clothing fibres.
Can I spray it directly on my skin?
No. ODORSTRIKE is formulated exclusively for clothing fabrics. Do not spray on bare skin, face, or eyes. If contact occurs, simply rinse with clean water.
Will it stain my clothes or leave white marks?
No. ODORSTRIKE is glycerine-free, propellant-free, and dries in 10 seconds leaving zero residue or staining, even on dark black or navy shirts.
How many sprays are in one 50ml bottle?
Each 50ml bottle provides approximately 250 measured sprays. At 2–3 sprays per use, that equals roughly 80–120 uses (2–3 months of daily use).
How long does the fabric protection last?
Up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under standard daily office, commute, or social conditions.
Does ODORSTRIKE have a scent?
ODORSTRIKE has a very subtle, clean, transient functional scent upon spray that disappears rapidly as it dries, leaving the fabric neutral without clashing with your perfume.
