Why this exists

Built after a bad
client meeting

Smelloff started with an hour-long ride through 41°C Hyderabad heat, a client meeting at the other end, and the moment a colleague's expression shifted.

Deodorant had done its job. The shirt hadn't. That gap — a fabric-specific fix built for Indian heat, humidity and commutes, at a price that isn't precious — didn't exist, so we spent six months and a pile of failed prototypes building it.

Questions, answered

What is Smelloff?

Smelloff is an Indian odor-control brand for men. It goes after smell where it actually hides — in the fabric of the clothes you wear — instead of covering it with fragrance.

Is Smelloff a deodorant or a perfume?

Neither. Deodorant works on skin. Smelloff works on the clothes your skin touches all day. It completes your routine instead of replacing it.

What does Smelloff make?

ODORSTRIKE — a pocket-sized fabric odor neutralizer for clothes. Shirts, tees, hoodies, jackets, blazers, jeans, trousers and uniforms. 50ml, around 250 sprays, ₹229.

Where is Smelloff made?

Made in India. Smelloff is based in Hyderabad and ships across India.

Does Smelloff go on skin?

No. ODORSTRIKE is made for fabric only — never skin, hair or body. It also does not replace washing and does not remove stains.
