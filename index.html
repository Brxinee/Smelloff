Before You Buy

Common questions.

Does Smelloff have a fragrance?

Yes. Smelloff contains a light fragrance that gives your clothes a fresh scent immediately after spraying. The scent is intentionally subtle and fades within minutes; the product’s purpose is odor control, not leaving a strong perfume smell.

Is ODORSTRIKE a perfume or deodorant?

Neither. Deodorant is formulated for skin bacteria. Perfume layers heavy fragrance over sweat. ODORSTRIKE is a fabric-only odor mist that gives a light fresh scent on spray while active odor-control ingredients (HPβCD + Zinc PCA) neutralize and trap sweat and clothing odor in the weave.

Can I use it on skin?

No. Fabric only. Not for skin, face, hair, or eyes. Keep deodorant for the body; this is formulated strictly for clothing.

Will it leave white marks or stain dark clothes?

ODORSTRIKE is water-soluble and dries clear — zero white chalky residue on dark fabric. Tested on black, navy, charcoal, and grey shirts across 30+ cycles: no marks, no stiffness, no discoloration. Patch-test silk, zari, and delicate weaves on an inside seam first. Do not use on leather, suede, or dry-clean-only garments.

Does it replace washing?

No. It is a between-wash fabric refresh. It does not remove dirt, body oils, or physical stains. Wash normally when the garment is visibly soiled.

How much should I spray and how long does it take to dry?

2–3 sprays for targeted collar/underarm refresh, 4–5 sprays for a full shirt front and back, and 5–6 for a jacket. Hold the bottle 15–20 cm away. It air-dries completely clear in approximately 10 seconds.

What fabrics is ODORSTRIKE safe on?

It is safe on cotton, polyester, rayon, linen, denim, and cotton-poly blends. For delicate fabrics like pure silk or zari embroidery, patch-test a hidden seam first. Do not spray on leather, suede, or dry-clean-only coats.

UPI pending transactions usually auto-resolve within 24–48 hours. If money is debited and stays pending beyond that, it auto-reverses to your account. If it doesn't, contact your bank — we cannot release a refund for a payment we never received.
