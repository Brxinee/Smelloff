07.1 // Editorial Principle

DON'T TAKE OUR WORD FOR IT. LOOK AT THE FORMULATION.

Most odor products rely on overpowering artificial fragrances to mask sweat smell. ODORSTRIKE uses functional host-guest inclusion chemistry and mineral zinc complexes engineered specifically for clothing fabric yarns — without harsh propellants, sticky residues, or heavy perfume oils.

07.2 // Transparent Formulation CLEAN AQUEOUS ACTIVE COMPLEX
01 // ACTIVE ENCAPSULATOR
HPβCD
HYDROXYPROPYL BETA-CYCLODEXTRIN
WHAT IT IS

A ring-shaped cyclic oligosaccharide derived from plant starch with a hydrophobic interior cavity.

WHY IT IS INCLUDED

Acts as a microscopic cage that physically traps and encapsulates volatile organic sweat compounds (such as isovaleric and butyric acids) inside its central cavity.

HONEST SCOPE

Traps volatile odorants on textile yarns so they cannot evaporate into ambient air. Does not alter your body chemistry or degrade fabric fibers.

02 // ACTIVE NEUTRALIZER
ZINC PCA
ZINC PYRROLIDONE CARBOXYLATE
WHAT IT IS

The physiological zinc salt of L-pyrrolidone carboxylic acid, widely used in advanced textile and personal care science.

WHY IT IS INCLUDED

Chemically complexes with short-chain fatty acids and volatile sulfur-containing odorants on fabric weaves, neutralizing malodors at their source.

HONEST SCOPE

Targets odorants deposited on fabrics without requiring thick synthetic masking oils or staining fragrances.

03 // DELIVERY MATRIX
FAST-DRY CARRIER
PURIFIED AQUEOUS SOLUTION
WHAT IT IS

A balanced, purified aqueous carrier system engineered for micro-mist mechanical spray pumps.

WHY IT IS INCLUDED

Disperses the active complex evenly across fabric fibers and evaporates cleanly in 30–60 seconds.

HONEST SCOPE

100% propellant-free (no LPG/butane). Leaves zero white crusts, stiffness, or sticky residue on dark or light garments.
