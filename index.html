FABRIC ODOR CONTROL · THE SCIENCE

Not perfume. Not deodorant.
Odor control for clothes.

Sweat and daily odors get trapped inside the weave of your clothes. Perfumes and body sprays only sit on top of the fabric and layer heavy scent over the smell. ODORSTRIKE targets trapped odor directly in the fibres — neutralizing it at the source instead of masking.

  1. 01 — TRAP

    HPβCD

    Hollow ring-shaped molecule physically cages trapped odor compounds inside fabric fibres so smell cannot escape.

  2. 02 — NEUTRALIZE

    Zinc PCA

    Zinc salt binds to captured odor compounds and neutralizes them directly at the fabric source instead of masking.

  3. 03 — PREVENT

    Triethyl Citrate

    Slows the breakdown of fresh sweat in fibres before new odor can form, maintaining up to 8 hours of fabric protection.

  4. 04 — ANTI-REGROWTH

    Zinc Gluconate

    Helps prevent odor from re-developing in garment fibres between wears so the reset holds throughout your day.

Questions, answered

Is ODORSTRIKE safe on skin?

No. ODORSTRIKE is formulated exclusively for fabric — never skin, face, hair, or body. Keep using your regular deodorant on skin; use ODORSTRIKE on the clothes that trap sweat odor. If accidental skin contact occurs, rinse with clean water.

Will it stain my clothes?

No. ODORSTRIKE is glycerine-free and dries clear with zero residue when sprayed from the recommended 15–20 cm distance. It leaves no white marks, stiffness, or oily film on dark or light clothes. Safe on everyday washable fabrics like cotton, polyester, denim, and wool. Patch-test silk, zari, and delicate embroidery on a hidden seam first.

How do I use it?

Hold the bottle 15–20 cm away, spray 2–3 light mists directly onto odor-prone zones (underarms, collar, or chest), wait approximately 10 seconds for the mist to dry, and wear. In high humidity, allow the fabric to dry fully before putting the garment on.

How long does the effect last?

Up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office and commute conditions. On high-heat days, heavy workouts, or extended commutes, reapply 1–2 sprays to high-friction areas as needed. One 50ml bottle provides approximately 250 sprays.

What fabrics can I use it on?

ODORSTRIKE works on everyday washable clothing fabrics including cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, wool, and synthetic blends. Do not use on leather, suede, or dry-clean-only garments. When in doubt on delicate fabrics, test a small inside seam first.

Does it replace washing?

No. ODORSTRIKE neutralizes odor molecules trapped in clothing fibres, but it does not remove dirt, oils, or stains. Think of it as an odor reset between wears to keep clothes fresh, complementing your normal shower routine and regular laundry.

Is the fragrance strong?

No. ODORSTRIKE has a subtle freshness signal designed to fade within minutes, leaving your clothes smelling clean and neutral. It targets and neutralizes trapped odor compounds rather than functioning as a perfume or heavy cologne.
