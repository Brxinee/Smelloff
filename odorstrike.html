Questions

Before you buy.

What is ODORSTRIKE?

ODORSTRIKE is a fabric odor spray for clothes made by Smelloff in Hyderabad, India: a 50ml pocket mist that traps and neutralizes sweat, food and smoke odor in the fabric instead of covering it with perfume. It is not a perfume, not a body deodorant and not a room spray. It dries in 15–30 seconds and gives up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office/commute conditions.

Is ODORSTRIKE safe on skin?

No. ODORSTRIKE is formulated exclusively for fabric — never skin, face, hair, or body. Keep using your regular deodorant on skin; use ODORSTRIKE on the clothes that trap sweat odor. If accidental skin contact occurs, rinse with clean water.

Will it stain my clothes?

No. ODORSTRIKE is glycerine-free and dries clear with no powdery residue when sprayed from the recommended 15–20 cm distance. It dries clean without stiffness, chalkiness, or an oily film on tested dark or light garments. Tested safe on everyday washable fabrics including cotton, polyester, denim, and wool blends. Always patch-test delicate silks, zari, or dry-clean-only fabrics on an inconspicuous inside seam first.

How do I use it?

Hold the bottle 15–20 cm away, spray 2–3 light mists directly onto odor-prone zones (underarms, collar, or chest), let the fine mist dry clean in 15–30 seconds, and wear. In high humidity, allow the fabric to dry fully before putting the garment on.

What fabrics can I use it on?

ODORSTRIKE works on everyday washable clothing fabrics including cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, and synthetic blends. Do not use on leather, suede, or non-washable garments. When in doubt on delicate fabrics, test a small inside seam first.

How long does the effect last?

Up to 8 hours of fabric odor protection observed under normal office and commute conditions. On high-heat days, heavy workouts, or extended commutes, reapply 1–2 sprays to high-friction areas as needed. One 50ml bottle provides approximately 250 sprays.

Does it replace washing?

No. ODORSTRIKE neutralizes odor molecules trapped in clothing fibres, but it does not remove dirt, oils, or stains. Think of it as an odor reset between wears to keep clothes fresh, complementing your normal shower routine and regular laundry.

Is the fragrance strong?

No. ODORSTRIKE has a light fresh fabric scent — not a perfume. It provides sensory confirmation of clean fabric without acting as a perfume or heavy cologne, settling close to the weave while active neutralizers target trapped odor.

Yes. Cash on Delivery is available across India. The COD total, including its handling charge, is shown on the Cash on Delivery option at checkout before you place the order. Prepaid orders are ₹229 with free shipping. COD orders are confirmed via a quick phone call before dispatch.

How long does shipping take?

Orders dispatch from Hyderabad within 48 hours of confirmation. Delivery takes 3–5 business days for major metros and 5–7 business days for tier 2 and tier 3 cities. Prepaid orders ship free nationwide.

What is the return policy?

We offer a 7-day return window from the date of delivery. The bottle must be at least 80% full and in its original packaging. Reverse pickup is arranged where serviceable, and refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.
