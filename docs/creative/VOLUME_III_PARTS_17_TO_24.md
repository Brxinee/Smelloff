PART 19 — EDITING PSYCHOLOGY & PACING RULES
================================================================================

Editing is visual rhetoric. The pacing must mimic high-retention human thought rather than polished corporate video production.

---

### CORE EDITING LAWS

1. **Jump Cuts Over Smooth Dissolves:** Never use cross-dissolves, slide transitions, or 3D page turns. Use hard jump cuts on action (e.g., cut mid-spray, cut on head turn) to preserve momentum.
2. **Pattern Interrupt Frequency:** Introduce a visual shift (angle change, zoom punch-in, b-roll insert, text flash) every **1.8 to 2.4 seconds**.
3. **Dynamic Punch-Ins:** Jump from 1.0x wide shot to 1.4x zoom punch-in on critical cognitive words (*"sweet-sweat", "two surfaces", "cotton yarn"*).
4. **Subtitles as Motion:** Subtitles must appear word-by-word or in 2-word clusters synced perfectly to the speaker's cadence. Never display full static paragraphs of text.
5. **Sound Effect Syncing:** Every visual text pop, cut, or graphic overlay must have a dedicated micro-SFX (subtle pop, click, or whoosh) to stimulate auditory attention.

---

### PACING RULES BY VIDEO LENGTH

#### 6-SECOND ADS
- **Cuts:** Maximum 2 cuts total (Cut 1: Hook action at 0.5s; Cut 2: Product proof at 3.5s).
- **Text:** One bold 4-word hook on screen; one persistent bottom CTA.
- **Audio:** Immediate spoken sentence starting at second 0.1; crisp mist SFX at second 3.0.

#### 15-SECOND ADS
- **Cuts:** 5 to 7 cuts total.
- **Timing:** 
  - 0–2s: Problem Hook (2 cuts)
  - 2–5s: Context / Agitation (2 cuts)
  - 5–9s: The Two Surfaces Aha (1 cut + punch-in)
  - 9–13s: Real-time Mist Demo (1 macro cut)
  - 13–15s: Final Offer Card (1 cut)

#### 30-SECOND ADS
- **Cuts:** 11 to 15 cuts total.
- **Timing:** 
  - 0–3s: Disruptive Hook (2 cuts)
  - 3–8s: Relatable Tension & Recognition (3 cuts)
  - 8–11s: The Perfume Mistake (2 cuts)
  - 11–15s: The Two-Surface Epiphany (2 cuts with whiteboard or physical demo)
  - 15–22s: Unedited Tactile Demo & Evaporation (2 cuts)
  - 22–27s: Sensory & Proximity Confidence Payoff (2 cuts)
  - 27–30s: Direct Offer & Pricing CTA (1 cut)

#### 60-SECOND ADS (FOUNDER & EDUCATIONAL BREAKDOWNS)
- **Cuts:** 20 to 25 cuts total.
- **Timing:** Use chapter-like pacing. First 10 seconds follow the standard hook curve. Middle 30 seconds dive into textile fiber biology and manufacturing truth. Final 20 seconds showcase demonstrations, price justification, and risk-free guarantees.

================================================================================

On-screen typography must guide the viewer's eye through an undeniable logical progression.

### THE 7-TIER ON-SCREEN TEXT HIERARCHY
1. **Hook Text:** High contrast, uppercase, positioned top-center. (e.g., *"DO NOT SPRAY COLOGNE"*).
2. **Problem Text:** Red or high-contrast accent, middle-third. (e.g., *"THE 3 PM SHIRT IS REAL"*).
3. **Aha Text:** Clean white with bright yellow highlight. (e.g., *"DEO = SKIN / THIS = SHIRT"*).
4. **Mechanism Text:** Clean sans-serif, lower-third. (e.g., *"~99% ACTIVE ODOR NEUTRALIZER"*).
5. **Proof Text:** Quantified, unarguable metrics. (e.g., *"DRIES CLEAR IN 10 SECONDS · ZERO CHALK"*).
6. **Offer Text:** High contrast, bold numerals. (e.g., *"₹229 PREPAID · FREE PAN-INDIA SHIPPING"*).
7. **CTA Text:** Action verb + direction. (e.g., *"CLAIM YOUR POCKET BOTTLE ➔"*).
