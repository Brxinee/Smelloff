### RULES FOR NATURAL, BELIEVABLE UGC SCRIPTING

Viewers on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts have developed instant cognitive immunity to "ad voice." If a creator speaks with manic influencer inflection or commercial jargon, the thumb swipes away in under 800 milliseconds.

#### 1. THE BANNED VOCABULARY LIST
- ❌ *"Hey guys, welcome back to my channel..."* (Immediate ad flag)
- ❌ *"Today I'm going to talk to you about..."* (Pre-roll YouTube energy)
- ❌ *"This revolutionary / life-changing product..."* (Overstatement destroys credibility)
- ❌ *"You NEED this in your life right now..."* (Presumptuous pushiness)
- ❌ *"Literally obsessed with this..."* (Hollow influencer cliché)
- ❌ *"Game-changer..."* (Meaningless buzzword)
- ❌ *"Don't walk, run..."* (Expired TikTok trope)

#### 2. THE 4 NATURAL CONVERSATIONAL LAWS
- **Law 1: Mid-Thought Entry:** Always start the video as if the camera turned on 2 seconds *after* the conversation already started.
- **Law 2: Vocal Hesitations:** Include natural human micro-pauses, breaths, or self-corrections (*"Look...", "Wait—", "I don't know why..."*).
- **Law 3: Lower Vocal Pitch:** Salespeople raise their pitch and speak fast. Trusted friends lower their pitch and speak with measured, conspiratorial intimacy.
- **Law 4: The Flaw / Vulnerability Lead:** Admitting something slightly embarrassing or counterintuitive builds immediate, unshakeable trust.
