PART 22 — COMPLIANCE, SAFETY & CLAIMS INTEGRITY
================================================================================

### 1. MANDATORY REGULATORY GUARDRAILS
- **Fabric-Only Classification:** Product must always be clearly identified as an apparel fabric spray, never a skin cosmetic, antiperspirant, or drug.
- **Zero Pesticide / Biocidal Claims:** Strictly avoid claiming that the product "kills 99.9% of bacteria," "sterilizes cloth," or acts as a medical sanitizer.
- **Physical/Chemical Odor Neutralization:** Frame the mechanism accurately: "Binds with, locks down, and neutralizes volatile organic odor molecules trapped in textile fibers."
- **Sensory Confirmation:** Clearly state that the fresh scent is noticeable and deliberate, providing sensory reassurance that the fabric has been refreshed.
