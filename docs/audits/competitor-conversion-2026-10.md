## Where Smelloff stands

**What this says**
1. **No Indian brand owns "odor spray for clothes".** The top fabric products
   have 22–81 ratings. They are 500ml home-fabric sprays, not pocket clothing
   sprays. The category is thin and winnable.
2. **The real pocket-size rival is the clothes perfume spray** (30–50ml,
   ₹149–282). Same pocket, same price, opposite job: it covers the smell.
   ODORSTRIKE's "neutralizes, not perfume" line is the difference, so the page
   should name that rival, not only body deodorant.
3. **Orders happen on marketplaces.** Every competitor above sells mainly
   through Amazon/Flipkart/Meesho, where the shopper already is, with Prime/COD
   trust and ratings. Small D2C sites (ArmourMe, Aquos) push buyers to Amazon
   ("Prefer shopping with your Amazon account?").
4. **Every listing anchors on MRP** (₹229 vs ₹499 is already in line).

## How Indian D2C stores convert (observed + GoKwik/industry data)
- **Prepaid incentive, cheap or free COD.** ArmourMe: 10% off prepaid, *free*
  COD, free gift above ₹999. boAt: 15% off UPI, ₹49 COD fee. GoKwik
  recommends 10–15% off prepaid; 5% doesn't move people. ~62% of Indian online
  shoppers prefer COD; COD RTO averages 20–25% vs under 2% prepaid.
- **Quantity offers**: "Buy 2 save 10%", "2 for ₹X" to lift a low AOV and
  spread the shipping cost.
- **Delivery date by pincode** on the product page (Aquos, Shiprocket stores).
- **Order on WhatsApp** for buyers who don't trust a new site.
- **Ratings everywhere**, usually from marketplace reviews.
- Avoided here on purpose: spin-the-wheel pop-ups, "X people bought today",
  countdown timers, fake MRP inflation (CCPA dark-pattern guidelines 2023).

## What fits Smelloff, what doesn't
| Idea | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pincode delivery check on the PDP | **Built** | Honest, uses the checkout's own window, removes a first-time-buyer doubt |
| "Order on WhatsApp" link by the buy buttons | **Built** | Low traffic means every conversation matters; founder answers |
| Name clothes perfume sprays in the compare table | **Built** | It's the real ₹149–282 pocket alternative |
| Prepaid option says it saves the ₹89 COD charge | **Built** | Same numbers, framed as the saving (boAt pattern); checkout only |
| List on Amazon.in (then Flipkart/Meesho) | **Owner** | Biggest order lever found; category has few reviews to beat |
| Lower the COD fee (₹89 is 39% of ₹229; peers charge ₹0–49) | **Owner** | Pricing call; weigh against RTO cost |
| 2-bottle price (e.g. "2 for ₹X") | **Owner** | Margin call; retired Duo ₹399 / Trio ₹549 must not return |
| Instagram creators / small Meta test to `/odorstrike` | **Owner** | Traffic, not site |
| Reviews from the 5 delivered buyers | **Owner** | Only route to stars; never seeded |
| Spin wheel, fake scarcity, "bought today" counters | **No** | Dark patterns; erode trust at ₹229 |
| Fragrance variants | **No** | ODORSTRIKE's point is not being a perfume |
