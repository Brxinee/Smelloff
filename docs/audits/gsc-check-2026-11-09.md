## Second pass, same day: drilldowns + the ProfilePage email

Owner sent four Coverage drilldown exports (two copies each of "Page with redirect" and
"Excluded by 'noindex' tag", data to 6 Nov) and the email "New Profile page structured

- **ProfilePage email: already fixed live.** All three issues are the markup before #585
  (merged 8 Nov 22:17 IST): a bare `ProfilePage` in the Person's `subjectOf` and
  date-only values. The live `/NikhilPatil` (build `2c6a227`, fetched with Googlebot's
  UA) has one ProfilePage, a Person `mainEntity` with name, image and sameAs, and
  `dateCreated` / `dateModified` as `2026-11-02T00:00:00+05:30`-style date-times. Google
  crawled the old version before the deploy; the email follows that crawl.
  Changed on top: `dateModified`, the sitemap `lastmod` and the visible "printed" date
  were the **build day**, so every shop deploy said the profile changed. They are now
  the day of the last commit touching `personal-site/content`, `src`, `assets` or
  `build.mjs` (`contentDate()`; today if git has no answer).
- **Excluded by 'noindex' (12 URLs): fixed.** `/odorstrike?buy=1`, `?buy=1&qty=2|5`,
  `?cart=open`, `?ref=launches.uicomet.com`, `/?buy=1|2|3`, `/?ref=producthunt` got
  `noindex` from `assets/js/track.js`, which rewrote the robots meta on any URL with a
  query. Those pages already point `rel=canonical` at the clean URL. Google's
  "Consolidate duplicate URLs" guide says not to use noindex for that within one site
  (canonical is the preferred signal), and a noindexed URL eventually stops passing its
  links, so the Product Hunt and uicomet `?ref=` backlinks were not counting for `/` and
  `/odorstrike`. The JS noindex is gone; these URLs will move to "Alternate page with
  proper canonical tag", which needs no action. `/payment-failed` keeps its noindex on
  purpose. `test/gsc-coverage-2026-11.test.mjs` pins it.
- **Page with redirect (91 URLs): nothing to fix.** Re-checked live with Googlebot's UA:
  90 answer one 301 to a 200 (www, http, trailing slash, `.html`, 29 retired post slugs,
  the retired `/solutions` pages). The 91st, `remove-smell-from-hoodie-without-washing`,
  is a live post again (restored 11 Oct) and answers 200; the export still shows its
  August crawl. No page, sitemap or feed links to any of the redirecting URLs.
