How to Make Clothes Smell Good

Fresh-smelling clothes are mostly about what you take away, not what you add. This guide covers the four steps that decide how a shirt smells, and what each laundry liquid actually does.

Quick answer

How do you make clothes smell good?

Take the odor out first, then add scent if you want it. Use the right amount of detergent, don't overload the machine, dry clothes fast in moving air, store them fully dry, and refresh worn clothes at the collar and underarms between washes. Scent on top of odor smells worse, not better.

A shirt can come out of the wash looking spotless and still smell flat, sour or faintly damp. Spraying perfume on it rarely helps: by lunchtime you smell the perfume and the old smell together.

Good-smelling clothes come from four steps done in order: wash, dry, store, refresh. Scent is the last and smallest part. Get the first three right and most clothes smell clean without any added fragrance at all.

Why do clean clothes sometimes smell bad?

Because odor can survive the wash: bacteria and body oils left in the fibres, or damp that sat too long before drying. Clean-looking is not the same as odor-free.

A Japanese study of laundry malodor (Kubota and colleagues, Applied and Environmental Microbiology, 2012) traced the classic wet-cloth smell of laundry hung to dry indoors to one bacterium, Moraxella osloensis, and one compound it produces, 4-methyl-3-hexenoic acid. The bacterium showed a high tolerance to drying and UV light, one possible reason it survives in laundry during and even after drying.

Sweat odor works the same way. Body oil and odor molecules build up at the collar, underarms and upper back, and fresh sweat brings them back. That is why a washed gym tee can smell again within minutes of warming up; the guide to clothes that smell again as soon as you sweat covers that case.

How do you wash clothes so they smell good?

Use the amount of detergent the label says, leave room in the drum, and wash worn clothes soon. More soap does not mean cleaner clothes.

  • Measure the detergent. The American Cleaning Institute says too much detergent or too little "can leave your clothes less clean than using the optimal amount". More isn't necessarily better.
  • Don't overload. The same guidance: "clothes need room to move." A packed drum leaves the collar and underarms barely rinsed.
  • Don't let sweaty clothes sit damp. A wet gym tee balled up in a bag for a day gives bacteria a head start. Hang it to dry before it goes in the basket.
  • Keep the machine clean. If everything smells sour, the machine may be the source; see why a washing machine makes clothes smell.

How do you dry clothes so they don't smell?

Fast, in moving air, and all the way through. Slow drying is where most "clean but smelly" laundry goes wrong.

Take clothes out of the machine as soon as the cycle ends and spread them so air reaches every layer. Sun and a breeze work best; indoors, use a fan and space between hangers. The US EPA's guidance on mould is to dry wet materials within 24–48 hours and to keep indoor humidity below 60% (ideally 30–50%); a load that stays damp for a monsoon day is in the range where musty smells start.

Sun-dried clothes smell good for a real reason, explained in why clothes dried in the sun smell so good. If yours smell musty even after drying, the guide to clothes that smell bad after drying walks through the causes.

Which liquid makes clothes smell good?

Fabric conditioner and scent boosters add scent in the wash; they do not remove odor. Pick the liquid for the job you need done.

The American Cleaning Institute describes fabric conditioners as making fabrics "feel softer and fluffier, reduce static and wrinkles, and often also add a pleasant scent", and scent boosters as beads that "add long-lasting fragrance". Both are about how a clean load smells. Neither is built to pull sweat odor out of a collar. For vinegar and baking soda, the vinegar, baking soda and softener guide goes into detail.

How do you keep clothes smelling good in the wardrobe?

Put them away fully dry and let the cupboard breathe. A wardrobe holds whatever goes into it, including damp.

  • Fold or hang clothes only when they are dry to the touch at the seams, cuffs and collar.
  • Don't put a worn shirt back between clean ones; air it on a hanger outside the cupboard first.
  • Leave a little space between hangers and open the doors on dry days.
  • Strong mothballs leave a smell of their own; see the almirah and mothball smell guide.

If clothes come out of the cupboard smelling stale even though they went in clean, why clothes smell in the wardrobe even when clean explains the usual causes.

How do you keep clothes smelling good while you wear them?

Refresh the three zones that hold sweat odor, between washes, with a fabric freshener rather than perfume. This is the step most routines skip.

A shirt picks up odor from your commute, lunch and the office AC long before wash day. Perfume on top of that mixes with it. A fabric freshener for clothes works the other way: it traps and neutralizes odor in the fabric.   1. Take the shirt off or hold it away from your body.
  2. Spray from 15–20 cm: 2–3 sprays on one zone, or 4–5 for a whole shirt.
  3. Let it dry, 15–30 seconds (longer when it is humid), then wear it.

What makes clothes smell worse?

Adding scent to odor, drying slowly and storing damp. Each one undoes the steps above.

  • Perfume or body deodorant sprayed on a sweaty shirt: it covers the smell for a while and can mark the fabric; see clothes perfume spray vs fabric odor spray.
  • Leaving a wet load in the drum: the fix is a rewash and fast drying, not scent.
  • Re-wearing a shirt that never aired: yesterday's odor is still in the collar.
Honest limits

A fabric freshener goes on clothes only, never on skin, hair or body. It does not replace washing, remove stains or fix a sour, damp load. Never use it on leather, suede or dry-clean-only clothes, and patch-test silk, zari and heavy embroidery on a hidden seam first.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I make my clothes smell good without perfume?

Wash with the right amount of detergent, dry clothes fast in sun or moving air, store them fully dry, and refresh worn clothes with a fabric freshener at the collar, underarms and back. Without odor, clean cotton smells good on its own.

Which liquid gives clothes a good smell?

Fabric conditioner and scent booster beads add fragrance during the wash. They do not remove odor that is already in the fibres, so they work best on a load that is properly washed and dried.

Why do my clothes smell bad even after washing?

Usually slow drying, an overloaded or dirty machine, or body oil left in the collar and underarms. Laundry hung to dry indoors can pick up the wet-cloth smell made by Moraxella bacteria.

How do I keep clothes smelling fresh all day?

Start with a clean, fully dry shirt and refresh it between wears with 4–5 sprays of a fabric freshener from 15–20 cm, letting it dry before you put it on.
