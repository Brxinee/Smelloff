Kapdo Se Seelan Ki Badbu Kaise Dur Kare?

Barish ke mausam mein kapde dhule hue bhi baasi, geeli si smell dete hain. Yeh guide simple Hinglish mein batati hai ki seelan ki badbu kyon aati hai, kab dhona hi padega, aur kapde kaise sukhayein taaki badbu bane hi na.

Quick answer

Kapdo se seelan ki badbu kaise dur kare?

Jo kapda khatta ya geela-sa mehekta hai, use dobara dhoo aur 24–48 ghante ke andar poori tarah sukhao: chhote load, hangers ke beech jagah, pankha aur khuli khidki. Almari mein sirf poore sukhe kapde rakho. Seelan ki badbu spray se nahi jaati; fabric freshener sirf sukhe, saaf kapdon ki halki baasi smell ke liye hai.

Mumbai ho ya Kolkata, monsoon mein ek baat har ghar mein hoti hai: kapde do din mein bhi poore nahi sukhte, aur jab sukhte hain toh unme se ek geeli, baasi smell aati hai. Pehnte hi woh smell aur tez ho jaati hai.

Yeh seelan ki badbu hai. Iska ilaaj khushbu nahi, sukhana hai. Aur jo kapda pehle hi badboodar ho chuka hai, use aksar ek baar aur dhona padta hai.

Seelan ki badbu kyon aati hai?

Kyunki kapde der tak geele rehte hain aur us beech bacteria badbu banate hain. Ghar ke andar dheere sukhne wale kapdon mein yeh sabse zyada hota hai.

Japan ki ek study (Kubota aur saathi, Applied and Environmental Microbiology, 2012) ne ghar ke andar sukhaye kapdon ki geeli badbu ko ek bacteria, Moraxella osloensis, aur uske banaye compound 4-methyl-3-hexenoic acid se joda. Yeh bacteria sukhne aur UV roshni ko kaafi had tak jhel leta hai, isliye sukhne ke baad bhi kapde mein reh sakta hai, aur kapda phir geela hote hi (pasina, nami) badbu laut aati hai.

US EPA ki seelan (mould) wali salah ke mutabik geeli cheezon ko 24–48 ghante ke andar sukha dena chahiye, aur ghar ki humidity 60% se neeche (behtar 30–50%) rakhni chahiye. Monsoon mein Indian gharon mein yeh mushkil hai, isliye sukhane ka tareeka hi sab kuch hai.

Barish mein kapde kaise sukhaye taaki badbu na aaye?

Chhote load, zyada jagah, chalti hawa. Kapde jitni jaldi sukhenge, utni kam badbu banegi.

  • Machine ka cycle khatam hote hi kapde nikalo; geela load drum mein mat chhodo.
  • Chhote load dhoo: stand aur rassi par jagah kam ho toh ek baar mein kam kapde.
  • Hangers ke beech jagah: kapde ek doosre ko chhuein nahi; moti cheezein (jeans, towel) kinare par.
  • Pankha aur khuli khidki: band kamre mein kapde din bhar geele rehte hain.
  • Detergent label jitna: American Cleaning Institute ke mutabik zyada detergent se kapde zyada saaf nahi hote.

Seelan wale kapde dobara dhoye ya nahi?

Agar khatti ya geeli smell hai, toh haan, dobara dhoo aur is baar jaldi sukhao. Spray ya perfume us smell ko nahi hatate.

Barish ke mausam ki poori English routine monsoon musty smell guide mein hai. Agar har load se badbu aaye, toh machine dekho: washing machine se badbu kaise hataye.

Almari ko seelan se kaise bachaye?

Sirf poore sukhe kapde andar, aur almari ko hawa. Ek geela kapda poore khaane ko baasi kar sakta hai.

  • Collar, cuff aur silai tak sukha ho, tabhi tah karo.
  • Almari ko thoonso mat; hangers ke beech jagah rakho.
  • Jis din dhoop ho, almari ke darwaze kuch der khol do.
  • Deewar mein nami ho toh almari ko deewar se thoda door rakho.

Almari aur khushbu ki baaki baatein kapdo me khushbu kaise laye mein hain.

Barish mein pehne kapdon ko fresh kaise rakhe?

Geela kapda pehle sukhao; sukhe kapde ki baasi smell ke liye fabric freshener. Geele kapde par spray mat karo.

Office pahunch kar shirt nami aur pasine se baasi lag sakti hai. Agar kapda sukha hai, toh ODORSTRIKE barish ke din ke liye pocket fabric freshener kaam aata hai: HPβCD (cyclodextrin) badbu ke molecules ko kapde mein pakadta hai aur Zinc PCA unhe neutralise karta hai. 15–20 cm door se collar aur bagal par 2–3 spray karo. Humid mausam mein ise sukhne mein 30–60 second lag sakte hain, toh poora sukhne do, phir pehno.

Honest limits

Fabric freshener seelan, khatti ya geeli badbu theek nahi karta; uska ilaaj dobara dhulai aur jaldi sukhana hai. Yeh sirf sukhe, saaf kapdon ke liye hai, kabhi skin, baal ya chehre par nahi. Leather, suede aur dry-clean-only kapdon par mat lagao; silk, zari aur bhaari embroidery par pehle patch-test karo.

Frequently Asked Questions

Barish me kapdo se badbu kaise hataye?

Khatti ya geeli smell wale kapde dobara dhoo aur is baar jaldi sukhao: chhote load, hangers ke beech jagah, pankha aur khuli khidki. Almari mein sirf poore sukhe kapde rakho.

Kapdo me seelan ki badbu kyon aati hai?

Kapde der tak geele rehne par bacteria badbu banate hain. Kubota ki 2012 ki study mein ghar ke andar sukhaye kapdon ki badbu Moraxella osloensis naam ke bacteria se judi mili.

Kya perfume ya spray se seelan ki badbu jaati hai?

Nahi. Seelan ki badbu dhulai aur jaldi sukhane se jaati hai. Fabric freshener sirf sukhe, saaf kapdon ki halki baasi smell ke liye hai.

Kapde kitni der mein sukh jaane chahiye?

US EPA geeli cheezon ko 24–48 ghante ke andar sukhane ki salah deta hai. Kapdon ke liye jitna jaldi, utna achha.
