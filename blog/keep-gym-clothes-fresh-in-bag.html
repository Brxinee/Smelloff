Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stop my gym clothes turning my bag into an odor box?

To stop gym clothes turning your bag into an odor box, separate the sweaty kit in a ventilated or waterproof pouch, get it out to air the moment you are home, and mist it with a fabric neutraliser rather than sealing the smell in. Leaving the bag itself open to dry between sessions helps too. Damp fabric plus a closed bag is what breeds the smell.

Why does my gym bag smell so bad?

A gym bag smells bad because sweaty clothes and shoes sit sealed inside, and the warm, damp, airless space lets odor bacteria multiply on the fabric within hours. The smell then soaks into the bag lining itself. Airing kit out promptly, keeping it in a breathable pouch, and neutralising the fabric with an odor spray stops the bag becoming a permanent odor source.

How do I stop sweaty clothes from making everything in my bag smell?

To stop sweaty clothes tainting everything in your bag, keep them isolated in a separate breathable or zip pouch so the moisture and odor do not spread to clean items, and unpack them to air quickly. Mist the damp kit with a fabric neutraliser to break down the trapped smell. Keeping wet gear apart and aired is what protects the rest of the bag.

Can I spray odor eliminator inside my gym bag?

You can spray a fabric odor eliminator inside your gym bag to freshen the lining, misting the interior lightly and letting it air and dry. It neutralises the odor bacteria leave on the fabric rather than masking it. Pair this with drying the bag open and not leaving damp clothes inside, since spraying works best once the source of moisture has been removed.

How soon should I unpack my gym bag after a workout?

Unpack your gym bag as soon as possible after a workout, ideally within an hour, because odor bacteria multiply fast on damp clothes left sealed in a warm bag. Hang the kit to air and mist it with a fabric neutraliser, then wash it when you can. The longer sweaty clothes sit packed, the deeper the smell sets into both the fabric and the bag.
