Why Does Polyester Hold Odor Longer Than Cotton?

The “polyester smell” is not just gym folklore. Fibre behaviour, moisture, odorants and textile microbes all influence what you smell.

Quick answer

Polyester can hold and release body odor differently from cotton because the fibres interact differently with water, skin lipids and volatile odorants. In a controlled study of 26 people after a fitness session, polyester shirts developed stronger, less pleasant odor than cotton shirts.

Why does polyester smell more after a workout?

Polyester is a hydrophobic synthetic fibre and its surface environment differs from cotton. It can interact differently with oily components of sweat and skin, while microbial communities on a textile can also differ. The result is a fibre–odorant–microbe interaction, not simply “polyester absorbs more sweat.”

What did researchers actually find?

Callewaert and colleagues compared cotton and synthetic shirts from 26 healthy people after an intensive spinning session. After incubation, polyester shirts were rated significantly more intense and less pleasant in odor. The study also found differences in bacterial growth patterns between the textile types. Read the study on PubMed.

Does cotton never smell?

No. Cotton can absolutely smell. It absorbs sweat and can support odor formation when worn repeatedly or left damp. The useful distinction is that polyester and cotton can retain, release and support odor differently.

Can washing completely reset polyester?

Sometimes, but not always. If odorants or oily residues remain after a wash, the next sweat event can make the smell obvious again. Repeated washing without addressing the textile’s odor history can therefore be frustrating with heavily worn synthetic sportswear.

What should you do with a clean polyester shirt that still smells?

Make sure it is actually clean and fully dry. Then target residual odor before the next wear. A clothing-specific odor neutralizer can be useful between washes, while persistent buildup may require a more thorough laundry reset.

The practical takeaway

Polyester is not “bad.” It simply has a different odor profile. Treat the fabric according to the fabric.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is polyester worse for body odor than cotton?

Research has found stronger odor development on polyester in controlled fitness-session studies, but actual results depend on fibre, garment design, wear and washing.

Why does polyester smell again after washing?

Residual odorants and oily residues can remain in the fibre environment and become noticeable again when the shirt is rewetted with sweat.

Can a spray remove polyester smell?

A fabric odor product can help with residual odor on a clean, dry garment, but it does not replace laundering when the fabric is dirty.

Does polyester hold odor longer than cotton?

Yes, typically. Polyester is oleophilic — it grips the skin lipids that odor bacteria feed on. Cotton absorbs water and releases residue more readily in a wash. That is why dri-fit and gym polyester smell again after a cold cycle.
