PRODUCTS & PRODUCT INFORMATION

ODORSTRIKE is formulated with active Zinc Ricinoleate, Hydroxypropyl Beta-Cyclodextrin (HPBCD), and Zinc PCA to trap and neutralize odor molecules embedded in fabric weaves.

MANDATORY USAGE GUIDELINES:

  • Fabric Only: ODORSTRIKE is designed strictly for clothing fabrics. It is not a body deodorant and not a cosmetic perfume. Do not apply directly to human skin, face, eyes, or mouth.
  • Material Suitability: Safe for everyday washable garments (cotton, polyester, linen, denim, poly-blends). Do not use on raw silk, suede, genuine leather, or items designated strictly as dry-clean-only.
  • Patch Testing: Always test on an inconspicuous inside seam before spraying delicate or bright-dyed fabrics.
  • Storage & Flammability: Contains ethanol as a fast-drying carrier. Keep away from open flame, sparks, and direct sunlight. Store below 30°C. Keep out of reach of children and domestic animals.
