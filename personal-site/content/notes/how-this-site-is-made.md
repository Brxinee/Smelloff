This site is meant to feel like a zine someone photocopied at the shop down the road: cheap paper, a ballpoint, a rubber stamp, a few cut-out pictures, and a person behind it. It's about me, so it doesn't borrow my startup's black and lime.

## Why a zine

Zines are what people make when nobody hired them to make anything. You write it, lay it out, copy it and hand it over yourself. That's roughly how I've learned everything since school, so it felt more honest than a portfolio.

## What it's built with

- Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework.
- A small Node script turns a folder of text files into these pages. Nothing on the page needs JavaScript to be read.
- Type: **Archivo** for headlines and text, **Courier Prime** for the typed bits, **Reenie Beanie** for the ballpoint notes. All three are open-licence fonts, served from this site.
- Colours: newsprint, toner black, ballpoint blue, stamp red and a highlighter.
- The photos of me are mine. The cut-outs (the boxing glove, the cassette) are free-licence photos from Wikimedia Commons, cut out with a short script. Credits are below.
- I build with AI coding tools, mostly Claude Code. I decide what goes in and check what comes out.

## What it doesn't do

- No cookies, no analytics, no trackers.
- No sound unless you press **Ring**.
- No motion if your device asks for less of it.

## Things to try

- Drag the things on the cover. **Tidy** puts them back.
- Press **Stamp**, then tap the cover.
- Pull the shutter in the top corner to turn the lights off.
- Press **Ring**. It's the sound my phone makes when someone orders from my startup.
