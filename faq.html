Product

Does Smelloff have a fragrance?

Yes. Smelloff contains a light fragrance that gives your clothes a fresh scent immediately after spraying. The scent is intentionally subtle and fades within minutes; the product’s purpose is odor control, not leaving a strong perfume smell.

What is ODORSTRIKE?

A 50ml pocket-sized fabric-only odor mist made by Smelloff in Hyderabad. Formula v3.1. Hero actives: HPβCD (trap) and Zinc PCA (neutralize). Not perfume. Not deodorant. Not for skin. ₹229 prepaid UPI for ~250 sprays.

Isn't this just a deodorant?

No. Deodorant is for skin. ODORSTRIKE is for clothes — collar, sleeves, hoodie, denim. Body odor that stays in the weave after a shower is a fabric job.

What is it not for?

Skin, hair and body — never. Not a stain remover. Does not replace washing. Not a room spray or air freshener. Built for the clothes you wear, in a 50ml pocket bottle.

How long does the effect last?

Up to 8 hours under normal office/commute conditions. After heavy gym or long heat, re-spray worn areas. Not a guarantee.

What's in Formula v3.1?

11 ingredients: Water, Isopropyl Alcohol, Triethyl Citrate, HPβCD (hydroxypropyl-β-cyclodextrin), Polysorbate 20, Zinc PCA, PE9010, Fragrance, Zinc Gluconate, Ethylhexylglycerin, Citric Acid. Concentrations are not published. See ingredient notes.

Use

How do I use it?

Hold 15–20 cm from clothing. 2–3 sprays for a targeted refresh, 4–5 for a full shirt, 5–6 for a jacket. Let it air-dry for approximately 10 seconds. In high humidity, wait until fully dry (up to 30–60 seconds). Then wear it. Full walkthrough: how to use ODORSTRIKE.

Does it replace washing?

No. It does not remove dirt, oils, or stains. Use it between washes on clothes that are still clean enough to wear.

Can I spray it on my skin?

No. Fabric only. Not for skin, face, hair, or eyes. If contact occurs, rinse with water.

Is it safe to inhale?

Spray on fabric, not toward your face. The isopropyl alcohol evaporates in approximately 10 seconds. Once dry, wear the garment. Do not inhale the mist directly.

Does it work in monsoon humidity?

Yes — that's a core Indian-use case. In high humidity, wait until the fabric is fully dry before wearing. Spray before heading out, or after getting caught in rain, on clothing fabric only.

How long does one bottle last?

One 50ml bottle is about 250 sprays — about 100 refreshes at 2–3 sprays. We do not convert that into weeks or months.

Fabric

Will it stain dark clothes?

No. ODORSTRIKE is water-soluble and dries clear — no white residue on dark fabric. Tested on black, navy, charcoal, and grey shirts across 30+ wash cycles: no marks, no stiffness, no colour change. Patch-test silk, zari, and heavy embroidery on a hidden seam first.

What fabrics is it safe on?

Cotton, polyester, blends, denim, and wool. Do not use on leather, suede, or dry-clean-only garments. Patch-test silk. When unsure, test on an inside seam first.

Can I use it on gym clothes / polyester?

Yes. Polyester holds odor. ODORSTRIKE is made for clothing fabric including gym tees and blends. It still does not replace washing a soiled garment.

Payment Failed / Failed Transaction Policy

At Smelloff, we accept payments via direct UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, QR code) and Cash on Delivery. Payments made via UPI are verified automatically.

1. Money debited but order not confirmed

If your UPI app shows the payment as successful but you didn't receive an instant confirmation:

  • Email us at smelloffsupport@gmail.com with your Order ID, phone number, and payment transaction reference.
  • We'll check the payment against our records within 24 hours.
  • If confirmed, your order status is updated immediately. If the payment failed to settle, we'll guide you or issue a full refund within 5–7 business days.

2. Payment failed at your end

If your UPI app shows "Failed" or "Pending" and no money was debited, simply retry. No action needed from us.

3. Payment stuck in "Pending"

UPI pending transactions usually auto-resolve within 24–48 hours. If money is debited and stays pending beyond that, it auto-reverses to your account. If it doesn't, contact your bank — we cannot release a refund for a payment we never received.

4. Duplicate payment

If you accidentally paid twice for the same order, email us with your Order ID. We'll refund the duplicate amount within 5–7 business days.

5. Wrong amount paid

  • Paid less: We'll request the balance before dispatching.
  • Paid more: We'll refund the excess within 5–7 business days.

Refund Timeline

All verified refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original UPI account. Bank processing may take an additional 2–3 days.
