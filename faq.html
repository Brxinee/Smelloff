What is ODORSTRIKE?

ODORSTRIKE is a clothing deodorant made by Smelloff in Hyderabad, India: a 50ml pocket fabric odor spray that traps and neutralizes sweat, food and smoke odor in the fabric instead of covering it with perfume, and leaves a light fresh scent. It goes on clothes, never on skin: it is not a body deodorant, not a perfume and not a room spray. It dries in 15–30 seconds and gives up to 8 hours of odor protection on fabric under normal office/commute conditions.

It works with HPβCD, a cyclodextrin that traps odor molecules, and Zinc PCA, which neutralizes them on the fibres. Use it on cotton, polyester, denim and wool blends. One 50ml bottle is about 250 sprays for ₹229, roughly ₹0.92 a spray. See how it compares in the best fabric odor sprays in India (2026).

Will it stain dark clothes?

No. ODORSTRIKE is water-soluble and dries clear without stiff residue on dark fabric when misted from 15–20 cm. Tested on black, navy, charcoal, and grey shirts across wear cycles: leaves no stiff marks or powdery film when properly dried. When unsure or on delicate fabrics, test on an inside seam first.

What actually neutralizes sweat smell on fabric?

Fabric odor sprays with Zinc PCA bind to odor molecules in fabric fibers and deactivate them at the molecular level — neutralization, not masking. Deodorant treats skin; it can't reach the odor trapped in your shirt. ODORSTRIKE goes directly on the fabric and dries clean in 15–30 seconds when sprayed as a fine mist from about 15cm. For the full day-long routine, see how to not smell sweaty at work.

Why do gym clothes smell after washing? Polyester is hydrophobic — water and detergent can't penetrate the micro-fibers where odor compounds hide. The surface gets clean; the inside stays rank. A fabric-specific odor neutralizer reaches where detergent can't.

Can I spray it on my skin?

No — ODORSTRIKE is made for fabric only (cotton, polyester, denim, wool). It's not tested for skin contact. Keep using your deodorant for that. Use ODORSTRIKE on what your skin already left on your clothes.

Isn't this just a deodorant?

It's a deodorant for clothes, not for your body. Body deodorant goes on skin; ODORSTRIKE goes on fabric. Sweat odor doesn't stay on your skin — it soaks into your shirt, jacket, and gym tee, and your morning shower won't fix what your shirt absorbed yesterday. ODORSTRIKE neutralizes those odor molecules in the weave. Keep using your own deodorant on your skin.

How is it different from Febreze?

Different approach entirely. Febreze uses fragrance to mask odor. ODORSTRIKE uses Zinc PCA and HPβCD (a cyclodextrin) to neutralize odor molecules at the molecular level — elimination, not cover-up, with only a light fragrance rather than a heavy scent. Priced at ₹229 vs Febreze at ₹300+. Formulated for Indian fabrics, sweat patterns, and humidity.

Can I use it on gym clothes / polyester?

Yes, and it's especially effective on polyester. Polyester is hydrophobic — water-based washing can't penetrate the micro-fibers where odor compounds hide. The surface gets clean; the inside stays rank. ODORSTRIKE's alcohol carrier reaches where detergent can't. It was built specifically for gym clothes and synthetic fabrics.

How long does the effect last?

Up to 8 hours under office or commute conditions, depending on the smell and bacteria level. In heavy heat, humidity, or after gym, re-spritz the collar and armpit zones. One 50ml bottle gives ~250 sprays — most daily users get 4–6 weeks per bottle.

What is it not for?

Skin, hair and body — never. ODORSTRIKE is made for fabric only. It is not a stain remover, and it does not replace washing: it neutralizes the odor trapped in the fibres, not the dirt. It's built for the clothes you wear — shirts, tees, hoodies, jackets, blazers, jeans, trousers and uniforms. It is not a room spray, not an air freshener, and a 50ml pocket bottle is the wrong tool for sofas or curtains.

Does it replace washing?

No. ODORSTRIKE neutralizes odor molecules trapped in fabric, but it does not remove dirt, oils, or stains. Think of it as an odor reset between washes — not a substitute for laundry. Use it to extend wear on clean clothes. Wash normally when the fabric has visible dirt or stains.

Is it safe to inhale?

Don't breathe in the mist. Spray it on fabric at arm's length, away from your face and other people, and let it dry fully (15–30 seconds) before you wear it. ODORSTRIKE is designed for fabric only, not for direct skin contact or inhalation.

Does it work in monsoon humidity?

Yes. Monsoon humidity accelerates bacterial growth in fabric — exactly what causes that damp, musty smell. ODORSTRIKE's Zinc PCA formula works in high humidity. Spray on clothes before stepping out or after getting caught in rain. Particularly effective in humid cities like Mumbai, Chennai, and Hyderabad during June–September.

What fabrics is it safe on?

Yes for cotton, polyester, denim, nylon, and wool blends — the fabrics most Indian wardrobes are built from. Do not use on leather, suede, silk, or dry-clean-only garments. When unsure, spray one pump on an inside seam, let dry, and check before spraying the full garment.
