Why Synthetic Gym Clothes Trap Sweat Smell

Unlike natural cotton, synthetic microfibre gym clothing acts like a magnet for body lipids and apocrine sweat. Body heat during workouts and commutes reactivates trapped odor molecules even after washing.

1. Oleophilic Fibers

2. Damp Between Wears
