Why Synthetic Gym Clothes Trap Sweat Smell

Unlike natural cotton, synthetic microfibre gym clothing acts like a magnet for body lipids and apocrine sweat. Body heat during workouts and commutes reactivates trapped odor molecules even after washing.

1. Oleophilic Fibers

Polyester absorbs sweat oils deeply into micro-grooves where laundry detergents struggle to penetrate.

2. Damp Between Wears

A tee folded away still damp keeps its bacteria alive, so the smell is already there next time you pull it on.

3. Active Chemical Neutralization

Zinc PCA binds sulfur and nitrogen compounds directly in the garment weave.

4. Pocket Portability
