Smelloff ("we", "us", "our") operates smelloff.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

## Information we collect

-   **Personal:** name, email, phone number, shipping address, and pincode — collected when you place an order or sign up for updates
-   **Payment:** processed via Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets) or Cash on Delivery. Card and UPI credentials are handled by Razorpay and never stored on our servers
-   **Account:** if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name via Firebase Authentication
-   **Usage:** pages visited, device type, browser, referrer — via Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel, using anonymised cookies

## How we use your information

-   Process and ship your orders
-   Send order confirmations, shipping updates, and delivery notifications
-   Provide customer support via email, phone, or WhatsApp
-   Send occasional marketing updates (only if you opt in)
-   Improve our website, product, and advertising through analytics
-   Comply with legal obligations (GST, consumer protection, etc.)

## Who we share it with

We share minimum necessary information with:

-   **Courier partners** (Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, India Post) for delivery
-   **Google Firebase** for authentication
-   **Google Analytics and Meta** for anonymised traffic analytics

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

## How payment works

Smelloff accepts payments online via **Razorpay** and through **Cash on Delivery**.

-   **Online (Razorpay):** Pay by UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), debit/credit card, netbanking or wallet through Razorpay's secure gateway. Payments are verified on our server before your order is confirmed. We never see or store your full card or UPI credentials — these are handled entirely by Razorpay.
-   **COD:** No advance payment. Pay the order amount in cash to the delivery agent on arrival (a small COD fee, shown at checkout, may apply).

## Cookies

Our website uses cookies for session management, analytics (GA4), and advertising (Meta Pixel). You can disable cookies in your browser, but parts of the site may not function correctly.

## Data retention

We retain order information for as long as legally required (minimum 7 years for GST records). You can request deletion of your account or marketing data at any time by emailing [smelloffsupport@gmail.com](mailto:smelloffsupport@gmail.com).

## Your rights under the DPDP Act 2023

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

-   **Right to access:** Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it has been processed
-   **Right to correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
-   **Right to erasure:** Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention obligations — e.g., GST records must be kept for 7 years)
-   **Right to grievance redressal:** Lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer (see below) and receive a response within 15 days
-   **Right to withdraw consent:** Withdraw your consent to marketing communications at any time by emailing [smelloffsupport@gmail.com](mailto:smelloffsupport@gmail.com)
-   **Right to nominate:** Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity

To exercise any of the above rights, email [smelloffsupport@gmail.com](mailto:smelloffsupport@gmail.com) with your request. We will respond within 15 business days.

## Analytics cookies and consent

We use Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel for anonymised traffic analytics. These are **non-essential cookies** and are only loaded after you give explicit consent via the cookie banner shown on your first visit.

-   If you accepted: analytics cookies are active. You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or emailing us.
-   If you rejected or have not yet chosen: no analytics or advertising cookies are loaded.
-   Essential cookies (session management, order state) are always active as they are required for the site to function.

## Security

Your data is stored on secure servers (Google Sheets, Vercel). Online payments are processed and verified securely through Razorpay. We take reasonable precautions but cannot guarantee absolute security against all threats.

## Children

Smelloff products are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
