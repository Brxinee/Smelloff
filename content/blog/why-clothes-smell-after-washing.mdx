## The polyester reservoir problem

Polyester is hydrophobic — it repels water but loves oils. The smelly compounds
from sweat are oily, so they cling to synthetic fibers and survive a normal
wash. That's why your gym tees and quick-dry shirts smell the moment you start
sweating again, even fresh out of the laundry.

<Callout>
Cotton releases odor compounds in the wash. Polyester holds onto them. That's
the whole difference.
</Callout>

## What helps

- Wash synthetics promptly — don't let them sit damp in a bag.
- Use a lower temperature but a longer cycle.
- Between washes, use a fabric mist that **traps** residual odor rather than
  masking it.
