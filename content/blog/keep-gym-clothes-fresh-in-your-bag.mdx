## Damp + warm + zipped = smell

A sweaty kit sealed in a bag is a bacteria incubator. Within hours, the odor
compounds multiply and embed deeper into the fabric. By evening, the whole bag
smells.

## The pocket routine

1. After your session, mist your kit lightly with ODORSTRIKE before it goes in
   the bag.
2. Leave the bag slightly open if you can.
3. Mist again before the next wear.

<Callout>
A 50ml bottle fits in the side pocket of any gym bag — about 300 sprays, enough
for weeks of sessions.
</Callout>

<CTAInline />

This keeps clothes wearable between washes — it doesn't replace washing heavily
soiled kit. For use on fabrics and clothing only. Not for application to skin.
