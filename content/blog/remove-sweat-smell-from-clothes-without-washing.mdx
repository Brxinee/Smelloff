<Callout>
Quick answer: Sweat itself is nearly odorless. The smell comes from compounds
that bacteria leave behind in the fabric. To kill the smell without washing,
you need something that **traps those compounds in the fabric** — not a perfume
that masks them.
</Callout>

## Why your clothes still smell

When you sweat, the sweat itself doesn't smell much. Skin bacteria break it down
into volatile compounds — isovaleric acid (cheesy), 3M2H (goat-like) and
thioalcohols (oniony). These molecules embed in fabric. Synthetics like
polyester act like a sponge, holding onto them and re-releasing the smell every
time you warm up.

## The 5-second fix

A fabric mist like ODORSTRIKE uses **cyclodextrin** — a ring-shaped, starch-derived
molecule with a cavity that captures odor molecules so they can't reach your
nose. Two light sprays on the collar, underarms and back, let it dry, and the
smell is gone. No washing, no perfume cloud.

## When you still need to wash

This refreshes clothes between wears — it does not clean them. Launder anything
heavily soiled or stained. Think of it as extending the life of a wear, not
replacing your laundry.
