PRIVACY POLICY

Data Governance & Protection
SMELLOFF // HYDERABAD

This Privacy Policy details the data collection, technical telemetry, and processing protocols governing your interactions across our digital platforms, purchase channels, and communication touchpoints.

DIRECTORY INDEX // DIRECT ACCESS
01 · Collection 02 · Purpose 03 · Processing 04 · Processors 05 · Cookies 06 · Comms 07 · Retention 08 · Rights 09 · Security 10 · Minors 11 · Amendments 12 · Grievance
01

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect only the technical, transactional, and contact parameters necessary to maintain digital operations and deliver requested campaign communications:

  • Submitted Contact Data: Valid mobile telephone numbers and email addresses provided when participating in campaign access sequences or registering for updates.
  • Transactional Records: Name, physical delivery address, billing details, and order identifiers generated during checkout sequences.
  • Automated Telemetry: Anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser user-agent tokens, referring URLs, screen dimensions, and navigation timestamps collected via server logging.
  • Communication Histories: Records of inbound support inquiries, WhatsApp status updates, and email interaction telemetry.
02

WHY WE COLLECT IT

Personal data is processed strictly under legitimate operational, contractual, and legal frameworks:

  • Service Delivery: Transmitting order confirmations, dispatch updates, and customer service notices via WhatsApp and transactional email.
  • Verification: Authenticating contact records to prevent automated bot entries and preserve platform integrity.
  • Order Fulfillment: Routing physical logistics, processing transaction receipts, and verifying delivery addresses.
  • Platform Security: Monitoring infrastructure logs to identify and mitigate malicious traffic, rate-limit violations, and security anomalies.
03

HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Your data is never sold, rented, or monetized with external data brokers. Information is utilized exclusively to maintain the active digital session, dispatch requested dispatches, process transactions, and fulfill regulatory compliance obligations.

04

SERVICE PROVIDERS & PROCESSORS

Data processing is conducted in coordination with vetted technical infrastructure partners:

  • Cloud & Database Hosting: Scalable cloud databases and compute environments for secure record storage and server execution.
  • Messaging & Email Gateways: Transactional communications infrastructure for WhatsApp notifications and automated status emails.
  • Payment Gateways: Authorized banking gateways for encrypted transaction settlement (we do not store raw card numbers or CVVs).
  • Logistics Partners: Domestic courier networks for physical package transit and address routing.
05

COOKIES & TELEMETRY

We utilize essential first-party cookies and lightweight client storage mechanisms strictly to preserve session state, track sequence completion, and ensure rate-limiting security.

You may modify your web browser preferences to decline non-essential cookies; however, certain visual state transitions and interactive elements may operate in fallback modes.

06

COMMUNICATIONS & OPT-OUT

You retain sovereign control over digital notifications. Every communication dispatch includes instantaneous opt-out mechanisms:

  • Email: Unsubscribe links positioned in the footer of every broadcast.
  • WhatsApp: Reply STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE at any time to immediately suspend automated sequence dispatches.
  • Direct Support: Transmit an opt-out directive to our support email at any time.
07

DATA RETENTION

Contact data collected for campaign sequences is retained for the active lifecycle of the campaign or until an opt-out/erasure request is submitted. Transactional and invoice records are preserved as mandated by applicable Indian taxation and accounting statutes.

08

YOUR DATA RIGHTS

Under applicable Indian data protection frameworks (including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you hold the right to:

  • Request confirmation regarding the processing of your personal information.
  • Request correction or rectification of inaccurate or outdated records.
  • Request complete erasure or withdrawal of previously granted consent.
  • Lodge a grievance with our designated data officer.
09

SECURITY SAFEGUARDS

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. All data transmission between your browser and our servers is secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Server endpoints enforce strict credential scoping and automated brute-force protections.

10

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our digital platforms, campaign sequences, and commercial offerings are directed strictly to individuals aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors.

11

POLICY AMENDMENTS

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy to reflect technical, operational, or statutory adjustments. The updated revision date will be reflected at the head and base of this document. Continued engagement with our services following revisions constitutes acceptance.

12
