01

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill user submissions, process authorized transactions, deliver communications, and secure website infrastructure:

Channel Data Categories Collected
Campaign Access Submissions Name, 10-digit Indian mobile / WhatsApp number, email address, submission timestamp, referral parameters (UTM tags), and affirmative consent status.
Storefront Orders & Transactions Full recipient name, contact telephone number, shipping address, city, state, postal PIN code, item selections, and chosen payment method.
Payment Verification UPI Transaction Reference ID (UTR / Reference number) and timestamp for automated or manual verification. Card numbers, bank credentials, and UPI PINs are never collected or stored.
Technical Telemetry Salted / hashed IP address (for rate limiting and fraud prevention), browser type, device operating characteristics, and access timestamps.
02

WHY WE COLLECT IT

Every category of data collected corresponds to a specific, legitimate operational necessity:

  • Campaign Dispatches: To send requested campaign briefings, updates, and milestone access notifications.
  • Order Fulfillment: To pack, route, and physically dispatch orders to your specified destination.
  • Transactional Notifications: To provide order receipts, real-time shipment tracking numbers, and delivery status updates.
  • Customer Support: To answer inquiries, troubleshoot issues, process eligible returns, or resolve delivery exceptions.
  • System Integrity: To prevent denial-of-service attempts, spam bots, and unauthorized automated API activity.
03

HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Personal information is processed in accordance with applicable Indian data protection principles, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules 2011, and the foundational notice and consent framework of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We do not sell, rent, or trade contact databases to third-party data brokers. Information is processed strictly for the operational purposes for which it was supplied.

04

SERVICE PROVIDERS

We engage vetted technical service providers to maintain website infrastructure and fulfill services. These providers have access only to the data necessary for their specific role:

  • Database Hosting: Supabase Inc. — provides secure, encrypted cloud database storage for records and transactions.
  • Email Services: Resend Technologies Inc. — handles transactional emails, receipts, and requested campaign updates.
  • Logistics Network: Shiprocket (BigFoot Retail Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) and partnered carrier fleets (Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, India Post, Shadowfax) — receive recipient contact and address details solely to route and deliver physical packages.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Google Cloud Platform — hosts web servers and application logic over encrypted connections.
  • Legal Compliance: Information may be disclosed where strictly mandated by applicable law, court order, tax audit, or governmental directive.
05

COOKIES & ANALYTICS

We maintain a minimal and privacy-conscious approach to website storage:

  • Essential Storage: Local storage is used for core functions such as preserving cart state, submission feedback, and consent preferences.
  • Cookieless Telemetry: First-party pageview and event metrics use temporary, salted hashes without tracking individual users across third-party websites.
  • Consent-Gated Analytics: Optional third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Meta Pixel / Conversions API) only operate when affirmative consent has been granted. You can clear your storage or manage preferences at any time.
06

COMMUNICATIONS

When you submit your contact details on the website or during checkout, you consent to receive direct transactional and campaign communications.

You retain immediate control over all communication channels:

  • WhatsApp Messages: You can opt out at any time by replying with the word "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE".
  • Email Updates: Every campaign email includes a direct, one-click unsubscribe link in the footer.
  • Direct Opt-Out: You may also email smelloffsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Opt Out" to have your contact details removed from future broadcasts.
07

RETENTION

Data is retained only for the duration necessary to satisfy the purposes for which it was gathered:

  • Campaign Contacts: Retained for the duration of the campaign lifecycle or until an opt-out / erasure request is received.
  • Commercial Transaction Records: Invoices, payment verifications, and delivery records are retained for at least 72 months to satisfy mandatory statutory accounting and tax (GST) requirements under Indian law.
  • Customer Inquiries: Retained for 180 days following resolution, after which they are archived or deleted.
08

YOUR CHOICES

Under applicable Indian law, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to request a summary of the personal data held about you.
  • Correction: The right to update or correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure: The right to request deletion of your information, subject to mandatory legal and statutory retention requirements.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: The right to withdraw consent for marketing or non-essential communications at any time.
  • Grievance Redressal: The right to have data-related concerns investigated by our designated Grievance Officer.

To exercise any right, submit a request to smelloffsupport@gmail.com from your registered email address.

09

SECURITY

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. All web communications utilize TLS encryption, and database access is restricted via role-based credentials. While we maintain rigorous safeguards, no digital system can guarantee absolute security.

10

CHILDREN

This website and its associated campaigns are intended solely for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from minors. If personal data of a minor is found to have been submitted, it will be promptly removed.

11

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in technical features, operational practices, or statutory requirements. Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
