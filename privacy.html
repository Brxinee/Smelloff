WHAT WE COLLECT

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with access to the 22.09 campaign, deliver product orders, communicate updates, and secure our systems. Depending on how you interact with Smelloff, this includes:

Interaction Data Categories Collected
22.09 Campaign Access Form Full name, 10-digit Indian WhatsApp mobile number, email address, campaign referral source / UTM tags, timestamp, and affirmative consent status.
Storefront Orders & Checkout Customer name, phone number, shipping address, PIN code, city, state, selected product quantity, and payment preference (Prepaid UPI or Cash on Delivery).
Payment Verification UPI Transaction Reference ID (UTR / Reference number) and timestamp for manual or automated verification. Note: Smelloff does not collect, handle, or store your credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs.
Technical & Browsing Information IP address (anonymized/salted for spam prevention), browser type, device characteristics, request timestamp, and essential session identifiers.
02

WHY WE COLLECT IT

Every category of collected data corresponds to a specific, legitimate operational necessity:

  • Campaign Notification: To send you the reveal briefing and priority access for 22.09 as requested by you when completing the access form.
  • Order Fulfillment: To pack, dispatch, route, and deliver physical ODORSTRIKE bottles to your designated shipping address across India.
  • Transactional Communication: To dispatch order confirmation emails, real-time shipment tracking numbers via courier integration, and delivery status notifications.
  • Customer Support: To address your questions, resolve shipping delays, assist with damaged shipments, or process eligible return requests.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: To protect our API endpoints against bot attacks, spam submissions, and unauthorized automated requests.
03

HOW WE USE IT

Your data is processed strictly in accordance with Indian data protection laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules 2011, and the foundational notice and consent principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We do not use your personal information for automated profiling that produces legal effects, nor do we sell, rent, or lease contact lists to third-party advertisers. Processing is restricted to service execution, lawful order compliance, and direct brand communications for which you gave explicit consent.

04

WHO MAY PROCESS IT

To operate our infrastructure and deliver products, Smelloff collaborates with trusted technical data processors under strict confidentiality agreements. We only disclose the minimum information required for each specific function:

  • Database Infrastructure: Supabase Inc. — provides secure, encrypted cloud database storage for order records, prelaunch access signups, and transaction tracking.
  • Email Transmission: Resend Technologies Inc. — delivers transactional receipt emails, order confirmations, and requested 22.09 campaign briefings.
  • Logistics & Courier Network: Shiprocket (BigFoot Retail Solutions Pvt. Ltd.) and partnered carrier fleets (Delhivery, Blue Dart, XpressBees, India Post, Shadowfax) — receive recipient name, address, PIN code, and mobile number solely to route and physically deliver packages.
  • Web Hosting: Google Cloud Platform / Cloud Run — hosts website files, secure API routes, and serves encrypted HTTPS web traffic.
  • Statutory Authorities: We may disclose information if strictly required by applicable Indian law, court order, tax audit (GST compliance), or governmental subpoena.
05

COOKIES & ANALYTICS

Smelloff utilizes a privacy-first approach to web analytics and storage:

  • Essential Storage: Local storage is used for essential site functions, such as remembering your cart state, consent banner status, and form session flow. These do not track you across external websites.
  • First-Party Anonymous Telemetry: We record lightweight, cookieless page performance metrics using salted, rotated hashes to prevent individual profiling.
  • Consent-Gated Third-Party Analytics: Optional tracking tags (Google Analytics 4, Meta Pixel / CAPI, Microsoft Clarity) are strictly gated and do not execute unless affirmative consent is granted. You can withdraw consent or clear your browser storage at any time.
06

WHATSAPP & EMAIL COMMUNICATIONS

When you submit your mobile number and email in the 22.09 campaign form or during checkout, you consent to receive direct communications regarding the 22.09 reveal and your order updates.

We respect your attention and maintain zero tolerance for unsolicited spam:

  • WhatsApp Updates: We send milestone notifications related to 22.09 and real-time shipping updates for orders. You can immediately stop WhatsApp messages by replying with the single word "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE".
  • Email Updates: Every marketing or campaign email includes a direct, one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. Clicking it instantly removes your address from future campaign dispatches.
  • Manual Opt-Out: You may also email smelloffsupport@gmail.com with the subject "Opt Out" and your registered contact details.
07

HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Campaign Signups: Retained until the conclusion of the 22.09 campaign lifecycle, or until you request deletion/unsubscribe.
  • Commercial Order Records: Under Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 and applicable Indian commercial law, invoices, transaction details, and delivery proofs must be retained for a mandatory period of at least 72 months (6 years) from the due date of filing the annual return.
  • Support & Inquiry Logs: Retained for 180 days following the resolution of your inquiry, after which they are purged or anonymized.
08

YOUR CHOICES & RIGHTS

You possess comprehensive rights regarding your personal information under applicable Indian law:

  • Right to Summary & Access: You can request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You may request correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal details.
  • Right to Erasure: You can request the deletion of your personal information (excluding records required by statutory tax and accounting mandates).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may withdraw previously granted marketing consent at any time without penalty.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer and receive an expeditious resolution.

To exercise any of these rights, email smelloffsupport@gmail.com from your registered email address with your specific request.

09

SECURITY

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3). Database access is strictly restricted through role-based access control (RBAC), environment secret isolation, and encrypted cloud storage.

While we maintain rigorous security protocols, no digital transmission or electronic storage system is 100% invulnerable. We encourage you to maintain privacy vigilance on your personal devices.

10

CHILDREN

Smelloff products, website experiences, and campaigns are intended strictly for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal information from children or minors under the age of 18. If we discover that personal information of a minor has been inadvertently submitted, we will immediately delete that record from our active databases.
